SUMMARY OF WATER USE-CASES OBSERVED
===================================
The lab tracks at least 7 functional water sub-types in the sample_name:

  1. Tap water for utensil washing       (most common — ~280+ rows combined)
  2. Filtered water for cooking
  3. Filtered water for dough mixing (العجانة)
  4. Filtered water for rice washing
  5. Tap/filter water for fish washing
  6. Basin / tank water (حوض)
  7. Generic filtered or tap water

In the official sample_category column, all 7 collapse to either:
  - "Tap water مياه الحنفية"  (chemistry)
  - "المياه الغير معبأة (Unbottled water)"  (microbiology)

If finer-grained water analytics is needed (e.g. is utensil-wash water more
likely to fail than cooking water?), use sample_name keyword matching rather
than the sample_category column.
